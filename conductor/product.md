# Initial Concept

To create and maintain an online presence for Biodinamična kmetija Černelič, promoting their ecological and biodynamic farming practices in Slovenia, likely including informational pages, potentially a gallery, and contact forms.

# Product Guide: Biodinamična kmetija Černelič

## 1. Vision

To create a comprehensive and engaging online presence for Biodinamična kmetija Černelič that serves as a central hub for information, education, and commerce related to their biodynamic farming practices. The website will build trust, foster a community, and drive sales by effectively communicating the farm's commitment to quality, sustainability, and the Demeter standard.

## 2. Target Audience

*   **Existing Customers:** Individuals already familiar with the farm who are looking for the latest news, product availability, and contact information.
*   **Potential New Customers:** Health-conscious consumers, families, and individuals interested in local, organic, and biodynamically grown produce.
*   **Biodynamics Enthusiasts & Learners:** People seeking to understand the principles of biodynamic agriculture, using the Černelič farm as a prime example of good practice (Demeter certified).
*   **Media & Partners:** Journalists, bloggers, and potential collaborators looking for information about the farm's unique philosophy and practices.

## 3. Goals

*   **Inform & Educate:** Clearly articulate the farm's history, biodynamic philosophy, and the importance of its Demeter certification. Educate visitors on the benefits of biodynamic food production.
*   **Showcase & Sell Products:** Provide a clear, attractive catalog of available farm products, facilitating customer orders and inquiries.
*   **Build Trust & Credibility:** Reinforce the farm's reputation by prominently displaying its certifications (especially Demeter) and sharing its story.
*   **Engage & Update:** Keep the community informed about farm activities, news, events, and educational opportunities through a dedicated news/blog section.

## 4. Key Features

*   **"About Us" Section:** A comprehensive area detailing the farm's history, its philosophy, the team, and in-depth information about the Demeter certification.
*   **Product Catalog:** An online catalog showcasing all farm products with descriptions, images, and availability.
*   **News/Blog Section:** A dynamic section for posting farm news, articles on biodynamic practices, event announcements, and educational content.
*   **Interactive Sowing Calendar:** A digital, interactive version of the Maria Thun sowing calendar to provide practical value to visitors and reinforce the farm's commitment to biodynamic methods.
*   **Contact & Order Form:** A clear and easy-to-use form for customers to place orders and make inquiries about products or educational programs.
