# Product Guidelines: Biodinamična kmetija Černelič

## 1. Prose Style & Tone

The website's prose will adopt a multifaceted approach, blending professionalism with warmth, and education with respect for nature.

*   **Informative & Educational:** Content will clearly explain biodynamic practices, the benefits of the farm's products, and the significance of the Demeter certification. The language will be precise and professional where technical explanations are required.
*   **Warm & Inviting:** The overall tone will be welcoming and personable, creating a sense of connection with the farm and its community. This will be evident in descriptions, blog posts, and general communication.
*   **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** The content will consistently reinforce the farm's expertise in biodynamic farming, its commitment to quality, and the credibility bestowed by its certifications. This will build confidence and trust with visitors.
*   **Respectful of Nature:** All language will subtly or overtly convey a deep respect for natural processes, the environment, and the principles of biodynamic agriculture.
*   **Homely & Welcoming:** Despite the professionalism, the tone will retain an approachable, "homely" feel, ensuring visitors feel comfortable and valued.

## 2. Visual Identity

The visual design of the website will be a harmonious blend of natural aesthetics and modern functionality, communicating the farm's ethos effectively.

*   **Earthy & Natural:** The primary color palette will draw inspiration from nature, featuring greens (representing growth and freshness), browns (earth, soil), and soft yellows (sunlight, harvest). Textures reminiscent of wood, soil, and natural fabrics will be incorporated subtly. High-quality, authentic photographs of the farm, its landscapes, and its produce will be central to the visual experience.
*   **Clean & Modern:** While natural in theme, the layout will be clean, uncluttered, and modern. Ample white space will be used to enhance readability and ensure a user-friendly interface. Typography will be clear and legible, contributing to a professional yet inviting feel.
*   **Traditional & Rustic Elements:** Subtle design elements may nod to the farm's heritage and traditional farming methods, perhaps through choice of fonts, minor decorative motifs, or historical imagery, without overwhelming the modern aesthetic. This will convey a sense of authenticity and rootedness.
